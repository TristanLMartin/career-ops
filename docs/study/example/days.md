# Study days

About an hour a day. Every bullet is one tickable item. Say every answer out loud.

## Phase A: foundations (Days 1 to 3)

### Day 1: your story
- Stories: write a 60-second "tell me about yourself" from cv.md
- Stories: draft three STAR stories in stories.md (a win, a conflict, a failure)
- Research: list five target companies and why each one

### Day 2: the role
- Practice: answer "why this role, why now" for your top company
- Practice: "Mock screen" in design-prompts.md, out loud, 20 minutes
- Stories: tell stories 1 and 2 out loud, under two minutes each

### Day 3: questions and close
- Practice: write five questions to ask an interviewer
- Practice: rehearse the pay conversation with your floor from config/person.yml
- Review: flashcards, flag anything shaky
